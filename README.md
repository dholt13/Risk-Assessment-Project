# Risk-Assessment-Project

An end-to-end cybersecurity risk assessment and compliance audit simulation evaluating a cloud-hosted patient platform against **NIST SP 800-53 (Revision 5)** security and privacy controls.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This cybersecurity risk assessment and compliance audit simulation evaluated a cloud-hosted patient platform operated by Apex Health Solutions, a fictional telehealth provider, against NIST SP 800-53 (Revision 5) security and privacy controls. Focusing on 15 high-impact controls across nine security families within their core Cloud Patient Portal and AWS/Okta infrastructure, the primary objective was to conduct a baseline risk assessment, identify critical technical and operational vulnerabilities, and showcase their overall security posture.

---

## 🛠️ Methodology & Approach

1. **Control Selection:** Utilized AI-assisted sampling to select 15 representative controls across key NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 families (Access Control, Incident Response, System & Communications Protection, etc.).
2. **Data Collection Methods:** Evaluated controls using three industry-standard assessment methods:
   * **Testing:** Hands-on verification of lockout thresholds, IAM roles, and firewall configurations.
   * **Documentation:** Auditing policies, SOPs, and system baselines.
   * **Interviews:** Engaging key stakeholders (Director of IT, Incident Response Lead, Cloud Architects).
3. **Disposition Mapping:** Categorized control status as *In Place*, *Partially In Place*, *Not In Place*, or *N/A*.
